# Kolibri 1.1 GGUF

GGUF quantisations of [Kolibri 1.1](https://huggingface.co/Aleph-Alpha/Kolibri-1.1),
Aleph Alpha's mixture-of-experts reasoning model for German and English. See
its [model card](https://huggingface.co/Aleph-Alpha/Kolibri-1.1) for
capabilities, intended use, training, limitations and documentation, and its
[blog post](https://aleph-alpha.com/en/blog/kolibri-1-1/) for what's new.

## Run with llama.cpp

Pick a quantisation that fits your VRAM or unified memory; the table below
shows what it costs in quality. We don't recommend running on CPU only.

```
llama-server --temp 1.0 --top-p 0.99 --top-k 128 -hf Aleph-Alpha/Kolibri-1.1-GGUF:Q4_K_M
```

llama.cpp can't load Kolibri 1.1 until [ggml-org/llama.cpp#32891](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/32891) merges; until then, build llama.cpp from that PR.
